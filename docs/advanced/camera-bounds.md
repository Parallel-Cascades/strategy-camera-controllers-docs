# Camera Bounds

Camera Bounds allow you to constrain the camera's movement to a defined rectangular play area, preventing the player from panning off the edge of the map.

All three camera controllers support camera bounds.

---

## Setting Up Camera Bounds

Camera bounds are configured directly on the camera controller component in the Inspector.

---

## Visualising Bounds in the Editor

When Camera Bounds are enabled, the controller draws a **gizmo** in the Scene view showing the bounds rectangle so you can quickly verify the extents.
